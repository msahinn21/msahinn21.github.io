Privacy Policy

Touchline Boss · last updated November 8, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Touchline Boss (“the game”) collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by Muhammed Şahin (“we”, “us”), an independent developer in Türkiye, who is responsible for it as the data controller. Contact: contact@muhammedsahin.net.

1. What stays on your device

  • Your career (club name, country, progress, purchases made, settings) is saved only on your device. We run no servers and never receive it. Deleting the game deletes it.
  • The Settings screen shows a random player ID created on your device. It is sent only if you e-mail us from the game, so that we can look into your problem.
  • The game has no accounts, no log-in, no chat and no way to share content with other players.

2. What third-party services collect

The game uses the services below. They collect information directly from your device under their own privacy policies.

Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (ads and consent)

We use AdMob to show ads (including the optional rewarded ads), measure them, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud. AdMob may collect your IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, an approximate location derived from your IP address and, only if you allow tracking on iOS, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). Where the law requires it, personalized ads are shown only with your consent. Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps.

GameAnalytics (game analytics)

We use GameAnalytics to understand how the game is played (for example the leagues reached, matches played, tutorial steps and purchases made) so that we can balance and improve it. GameAnalytics collects device information (model, operating system, language, app version), a random user identifier, session times, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and gameplay events; it collects the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking. The game sends no club names or other text you type. GameAnalytics Privacy Policy.

Apple (in-app purchases)

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store (the game uses Unity In-App Purchasing on your device to talk to it). We never see your payment details; the game only receives the confirmation it needs to deliver what you bought. Apple Privacy Policy.

3. Tracking and your choices

  • Tracking (iOS). The game asks for your permission before your advertising identifier can be used to track you. If you decline, it isn't shared. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland). A consent form asks for your choices about personalized ads and related data processing. You can change them at any time with Privacy Settings in the game's Settings.
  • No Ads. Buying No Ads removes the ads that interrupt play; the optional rewarded ads stay available if you want their rewards.

4. Legal bases

Where the GDPR, the UK GDPR or Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698) applies, the third-party processing above relies on your consent (personalized ads, tracking) or on legitimate interests (non-personalized ads, fraud prevention, analytics to keep the game working and balanced).

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country). We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has provided personal information, contact us and we'll help remove it.

6. Keeping and protecting data

Data on your device stays until you delete the game. The third-party services keep data under their own retention policies and safeguards, and may process it outside your country (for example in the United States or the European Union).

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, KVKK or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. As we hold no data on servers of our own, most requests concern the services above: e-mail us and we'll help, or contact them directly. We do not sell personal information. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority).

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the current version; significant changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.
