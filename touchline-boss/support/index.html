Support

Write to contact@muhammedsahin.net. The quickest way is Support in the game's Settings: it adds your player ID and device to the e-mail. We usually reply within a few days.

Questions

I bought No Ads but I still see ads

No Ads removes the ads that interrupt play; the rewarded ads you choose to watch for rewards stay. On a new device or after reinstalling, open the shop and tap Restore Purchase.

A purchase didn't arrive

Close and reopen the game: a purchase that was interrupted is delivered when the game starts. If it still hasn't arrived, e-mail us with your player ID and the receipt from Apple. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Deleting the game deletes your progress; purchases of No Ads can be restored.

How do I change my privacy choices?

Tracking: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): Privacy Settings in the game's Settings. See the Privacy Policy for what is collected.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the game to remove everything stored on your device. For data held by our advertising and analytics partners, e-mail us and we'll help.
